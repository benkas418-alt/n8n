# Brand film, founder story, app / SaaS promo

## Brand story arc (15–30 s, several shots)

1. **World**: the place and the people before (a market, a street, a family, a shop).
2. **Tension**: the everyday problem (waiting, distance, lost time, unreliable supply).
3. **Turn**: the brand appears as a gesture, not a logo shot (a courier arrives, an app opens).
4. **New normal**: the same people, better day.
5. **Signature**: one calm closing image with room for the logo and line added in editing.

Use 2.5 for 20–30 s single takes, or chain 2.0 shots with start/end frames
(`references/storyboard-series.md`). Keep faces and places local and true to the market:
real streets, real light, real clothing; avoid generic stock-photo settings.

## App or SaaS promo (motion design)

Do not ask the model to render a real interface: screens come out unreadable. Options:

- Generate the **context** (hands, phone, place, reaction) with a blank or blurred screen,
  then composite real screenshots in editing.
- Or generate abstract **motion design**: shapes, light trails, cards sliding, depth, in the
  brand colours, as background for real UI and text added later.

Pattern:

```text
8 s, 16:9, clean 3D motion design, brand colours [hex or names].
0–3 s: floating rounded cards assemble from the left. Camera: slow dolly in.
3–6 s: cards align into a phone silhouette, soft glow. Camera: slight orbit.
6–8 s: everything settles, large empty area on the right for the headline.
Sound: soft whooshes, light clicks, minimal electronic music.
```

## Founder / testimonial

Only with the real person's agreement and their real words. A generated "founder" or
"client" presented as real misleads customers; label any dramatisation.
