# Camera, light and sound vocabulary

## Light

soft key light · hard light · backlight / rim light (separates subject, shows steam and hair)
· golden hour · blue hour · overcast diffuse · neon practicals · window light · candle light
· high key (bright, commercial) · low key (dark, dramatic) · volumetric light / god rays ·
light sweep across the product · colour contrast (warm subject, cool background).

## Motion qualities

slow motion (liquids, hair, fabric) · real-time · speed ramp (fast then slow on the key
moment) · hold / freeze on the hero frame · seamless loop (end frame matches start).

## Transitions inside one clip

match cut on a shape · whip pan · object passes in front of the lens · push through a
surface · light flash · day-to-night change.

## Sound (native audio)

ambience (market noise, rain, city traffic, kitchen) · foley (pour, sizzle, click, zip,
footsteps) · music mood (upbeat afrobeat, calm piano, tense drums) · dialogue with the
language stated and the exact line in quotes · silence before an impact.

For French, Swahili, Lingala or English lines, write the exact words and keep them short:
one sentence per beat. Check pronunciation in the result; redo with simpler wording if
needed.
