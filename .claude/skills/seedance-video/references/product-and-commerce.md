# Product, e-commerce, food, fashion

## What sells in a product video

1. A **hero moment**: the product alone, clean light, sharp, centred, 1–2 s minimum.
2. **Proof of use**: a hand, a person, a real place. People buy the result, not the object.
3. **Touch for the eyes**: macro on texture, condensation, fabric, steam, crunch.
4. **One benefit per video**. Three benefits = three videos (and an A/B test).
5. End frame with **empty space** for price, offer and call to action added in editing.

## Structures (pick one)

| Structure | Beats (12 s) |
|---|---|
| Problem → solution | 0–3 problem visible · 3–6 product enters · 6–10 result · 10–12 hero |
| Reveal | 0–3 darkness/box/cloth · 3–7 reveal with light sweep · 7–12 slow orbit hero |
| 360 showcase | 0–12 continuous orbit or turntable, light moving across surfaces, ends front-facing |
| Unboxing | 0–3 hands on package · 3–7 opening, product rises into light · 7–12 in-hand |
| Ingredient story (food, cosmetics) | 0–4 raw ingredients in motion · 4–8 transformation · 8–12 final product |
| Lookbook (fashion) | 0–4 walk toward camera · 4–8 turn, fabric moves · 8–12 detail + full look |
| Delivery promise | 0–3 order on phone · 3–8 courier through the city · 8–12 doorstep handover, smile |

## Prompt pattern

```text
10 s, 9:16, photoreal product commercial.
Subject: the product from references 1–3 (exact shape, colours and label), on [surface].
0–3 s: [hook action]. Camera: [move].
3–7 s: [use / reveal]. Camera: [move].
7–10 s: hero shot, product centred, top third empty. Camera: slow push-in, then hold.
Light: [soft key from left, warm rim, …]. Setting: [place].
Sound: [ambience], [effect on the reveal], music [mood], no voice.
Keep the label and proportions identical to the references. No on-screen text.
```

## Example (PitUp, Lubumbashi)

```text
12 s, 9:16, photoreal, warm late-afternoon light.
0–3 s: close-up of a hand tapping "Commander" on a phone, a busy Lubumbashi street blurred behind.
3–8 s: tracking shot following a PitUp courier on a motorbike through traffic, orange delivery box
on the back (colour and logo from reference 1).
8–12 s: at a front door, the courier hands over a bag; the customer smiles. Camera slowly pushes in
on the bag, top third of the frame left empty.
Sound: street ambience, engine passing, short cheerful music sting at the handover. No text.
```

## Food

Steam, sauce pour, cheese pull, crunch, condensation on drinks: one sensory action per
shot, macro lens, backlight to show steam, sound effects on. Avoid hands eating in close-up
(distortions); show the bite result instead.

## Fashion and beauty

Fabric movement sells: wind, walk, turn. Keep the model identity from references; same
outfit block in every shot prompt. Beauty: before/after only if it is honest (no fake skin
results presented as real).
