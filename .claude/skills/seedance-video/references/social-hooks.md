# Social media hooks (TikTok, Reels, Shorts)

The first 1–2 seconds decide if people stop scrolling. Generate the hook as its own short
shot (4–5 s) and test several hooks with the same body.

## Hook types

| Hook | Image in the first second |
|---|---|
| Pattern break | Something unexpected moves into a calm frame (object drops, splash, sudden zoom) |
| Curiosity | A covered object, a door half open, a reaction before the reason |
| Result first | The finished dish, the clean room, the happy customer, then rewind |
| Direct address | Someone looks into the lens and gestures (UGC style) |
| Satisfying | Pour, cut, peel, perfect fit, ASMR sound |
| Contrast | Split moment: before on the left, after on the right |
| Speed | Whip pan or fast push-in straight onto the subject |

## Rules for vertical video

- 9:16, subject in the centre third, nothing important in the top 15 % and bottom 25 %
  (app buttons and captions cover them).
- Movement from the first frame; no slow fade-in.
- Native audio on: an effect on the hook moment helps retention.
- 8–15 s total for ads; loopable endings (last frame close to the first) increase replays.
- Captions, price and call to action are added in editing, not generated.

## UGC style

"Handheld phone footage, natural window light, slight shake, person speaking to camera in
French, casual home setting". Use a real person's face only with consent; label sponsored
content as advertising where the platform requires it.

## Prompt pattern (hook shot)

```text
5 s, 9:16, [style].
0–1.5 s: [hook image with immediate motion]. Camera: [fast move or handheld].
1.5–5 s: [first information: what it is / why care]. Camera: [move].
Sound: [effect on the hook], [ambience]. No on-screen text.
```
